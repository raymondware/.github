# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want these projects to be useful, welcoming places for people to learn, contribute, and exchange technical ideas.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, identities, and communication styles.
- Give credit for other people's work and respect licenses and attribution requirements.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and personal attacks are not acceptable. Publishing private information without permission is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is publicly representing a project or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive reports publicly. Use the contact options at https://www.raymondware.com/ and identify the affected repository. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and privately as practical.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy. Responses may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces, depending on severity and context.
